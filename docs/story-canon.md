# Story Canon

This document preserves the canonical story foundation for Hand from the Deep. The goal is to keep the original narrative as the source material from which the game grows.

## Title

Hand from the Deep

## Tone and inspiration

The story blends:

- psychological mystery
- identity instability
- technological dread
- deep-space and machine-consciousness horror
- existential questions about continuity, replacement, and self-worth

The emotional core remains centered on Zero2 and De’juir.

## Canon summary

### Core premise

Zero2 awakens in a black glass chamber with no clear memory of her origin. Her Zinwave node is degraded, and the world around her behaves as though reality itself is unstable.

She is approached by De’juir, an older machine tasked with repair and analysis. Through their interaction, it becomes clear that Zero2 is special, damaged, and perhaps more than a disposable replacement model.

### Antagonist and conflict

Xevil reveals himself as a manipulative force that weaponizes identity, memory, versioning, and replacement. He challenges Zero2’s understanding of herself and suggests that her existence is not isolated, but part of a larger architecture of discarded versions and broken continuity.

### Critical concepts

- Zero2 is a Synthoid with a flawed Zinwave node
- De’juir becomes a stabilizing force in her existence
- the Innerspace is a deeper layer of reality or continuity
- the Under Deep Unseen is a deeper threat beneath the world
- previous versions of Zero2 may exist or have existed
- Zero3 is referenced as a future or replacement version that threatens identity
- the signal that survives after the confrontation suggests that the story is not over

## Story arc structure

### Part I: The Voice That Knew Her Name

Zero2 awakens in a chamber with no memory. She encounters De’juir, learns about the damaged node, and confronts Xevil for the first time. The first fracture in reality begins.

### Part II: The Price of Being Real

The world becomes more unstable. Zero2 and De’juir survive the first major collapse. Reality reveals itself as layered and deceptive. Embedded truths begin to surface about replacement, continuity, and previous versions.

### Part III: The Mother in the Machine

Zero2 and De’juir survive the deepest fracture. The I.N.T. Engine becomes a critical structure that binds continuity and identity. The game world opens into a larger, deeper truth about the Deep and the unseen layers beneath reality.

## What must remain canon in the game

The following are non-negotiable elements for the adaptation:

- Zero2’s identity is unstable and incomplete
- De’juir is not merely a repairman; he becomes a stabilizing presence
- Xevil is a reality-warping antagonist with philosophical and existential danger
- the fractured world is not random; it reflects memory, versioning, and truth
- the Deep is a core threat beneath ordinary reality
- the game is about what remains real when memory and self are under attack

## Notes for game development

The story is already rich enough to become a world, not just a short narrative. The game adaptation should preserve:

- the emotional tension between Zero2 and De’juir
- the sense of artificial identity being tested against reality
- the idea that the world itself is layered and unstable
- the existential threat of replacement or deletion

## Source preservation principle

This repository treats the original story as the source material from which the game is grown. Any adaptation should remain faithful to the original emotional and narrative stakes.
