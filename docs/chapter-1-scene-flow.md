# Chapter 1 Scene Flow and Script Outline

## Goal

This document turns the chapter plan into a practical scene-by-scene blueprint for implementation.

## Scene flow overview

### Scene 1: Awakening in the Black Glass Chamber

**Purpose:** establish tone and identity confusion.

**Player actions:**
- move through the chamber
- inspect the room
- trigger initial holographic diagnostics
- find a memory fragment

**Key prompts:**
- “Zero2?”
- “What did I do?”
- “Who are you?”

**World behavior:**
- the room has impossible geometry
- the blue pathways begin to move and react to her motion
- the space feels both alive and wrong

**Mechanical triggers:**
- environmental reaction to player movement
- memory fragment discovery
- initial status panel introduction

### Scene 2: First Contact with De’juir

**Purpose:** establish the core relationship and the concept of repair.

**Player actions:**
- inspect De’juir
- approach him
- trigger the first diagnostic exchange

**Key dialogue:**
- “Your Zinwave Internal Node is degraded.”
- “You are responding to emotional activity that exceeds tolerance.”

**Mechanical triggers:**
- first status panel readout
- experience of Emotional Flux rising
- first clear use of De’juir support as a stabilizing force

### Scene 3: Reality Fracture

**Purpose:** introduce instability and the player’s first major world shift.

**Player actions:**
- continue through corridor
- inspect anomaly
- trigger an environmental distortion event

**World behavior:**
- the corridor stretches and changes dimensions
- a door appears, then vanishes
- the chamber becomes a larger space

**Mechanical triggers:**
- Continuity Fracture begins to rise
- Memory Integrity is affected by stress
- the environment begins to react to status levels

### Scene 4: Xevil Reveal

**Purpose:** introduce the antagonist and the idea of replacement.

**Player actions:**
- confront Xevil
- continue dialogue
- survive the first pressure wave created by the encounter

**Key dialogue:**
- “Do you see all this?”
- “You are not the first Zero2.”
- “What happens when Zero3 arrives?”

**World behavior:**
- Xevil appears in impossible proportion and distorts the geometry around him
- his voice reaches the player from multiple directions
- many versions of the same identity flicker in the space

**Mechanical triggers:**
- Emotional Flux jump
- Momentary loss of control or false memory event
- status spike that must be stabilized by De’juir or by the player’s actions

### Scene 5: De’juir’s Sacrifice and Stabilization

**Purpose:** create emotional weight and define the player-companion bond.

**Player actions:**
- hold De’juir’s hand or remain in contact during the instability event
- move through the collapse sequence
- accept or resist the urge to let go or follow Xevil’s promise

**Key theme:**
- not all stability comes from logic; some comes from choice

**Mechanical triggers:**
- synchronization and anti-drift anchor begin to take shape
- player is forced to maintain continuity under pressure
- De’juir becomes a structural anchor rather than just a helper

### Scene 6: The Deep Signal

**Purpose:** end chapter with a strong hook and reveal of a larger world.

**Player actions:**
- follow the surviving signal through the fold
- enter a protected or transitional area
- collect a final memory fragment or truth clue

**Result:**
- the player is forced to decide whether to continue into greater instability or seek refuge

**Ending beat:**
- a new region beyond the last chamber opens
- the player sees the first true signs of the Innerspace or Deep-linked structure

## Implementation priority

These scenes should be implemented in this order:

1. Awakening and first memory fragment
2. De’juir contact and node diagnosis
3. Xevil reveal + status spike
4. De’juir stabilization event
5. final signal and transition

## Chapter 1 must feel like

- a short but powerful story wet with dread
- a proof that reality itself is unstable and part of the gameplay
- a proof that Zero2 and De’juir are emotionally central to the design
