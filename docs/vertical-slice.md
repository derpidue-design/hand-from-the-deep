# Vertical Slice Plan

## Goal

Build a playable prototype that proves the game concept works and captures the story’s emotional and horror tone.

## Playable slice scope

This slice focuses on the first chapter of the story and should deliver a representative preview of the full game.

### Core playable sequence

1. Zero2 awakens in the Black Glass Chamber
2. The player learns basic movement and environmental interaction
3. De’juir introduces the first repair and diagnostic sequence
4. The player is forced to confront the first unstable reality event
5. Xevil appears and destabilizes the environment
6. The player experiences a memory fracture and must manage a status spike
7. The player escapes into a degraded corridor transition
8. The chapter ends on a strong hook leading into the deeper story

## Gameplay loop

The vertical slice should include:

- exploration of a small but psychologically unsettling area
- a simple interaction system
- memory fragments that clarify identity
- a low-complexity combat or survival encounter
- a clear bond mechanic with De’juir
- at least one major narrative branch decision
- a short progression arc ending in a meaningful reveal

## Required systems for the prototype

### 1. Basic movement and interaction

The player should be able to:

- move through corridors and chambers
- inspect objects and interact with diagnostics
- collect memory fragments
- trigger simple conversation with De’juir
- react to shifting environments

### 2. Status system demonstration

The prototype must show all four foundational statuses in a readable way:

- Zinwave Stability
- Emotional Flux
- Memory Integrity
- Continuity Fracture

These statuses should visibly affect:

- perception and environment behavior
- interaction success
- enemy or hazard pressure
- the final encounter logic

### 3. Companion support

De’juir should be present as a support system that can:

- stabilize Zero2 in key moments
- offer advice and narrative context
- help reduce instability during a dangerous event
- become a tactical or reactive support in a small encounter

### 4. Encounter pressure

The first enemy encounter should not be a full combat system; it should communicate the larger game’s tension.

Possible encounter types:

- corrupted synthetic entity
- memory parasite
- reality-warped hostile figure
- a pressure event where the player must survive instability rather than simply defeat enemies

## Scene structure

### Scene 1: chamber awakening

- player is dropped into the chamber
- no direct objective beyond exploration and observation
- memory fragments reveal missing facts

### Scene 2: first diagnostic interaction

- De’juir appears
- the player learns the basics of the Zinwave node
- the first emotional fracture begins

### Scene 3: reality shift

- environment becomes unstable
- the corridor begins to warp
- player sees evidence that the world is not fixed

### Scene 4: Xevil introduction

- Xevil appears as a distorted, manipulative force
- the player is threatened by identity fracture rather than normal combat
- a status spike presence should be obvious

### Scene 5: memory recovery spike

- the player recovers a fragment that suggests prior versions or a larger structure
- emotional pressure climbs
- De’juir helps stabilize the player

### Scene 6: escape and reveal

- the chapter resolves with a transition into a deeper truth
- the player reaches a new region or deeper fold of reality
- the prototype ends on a strong hook

## Success criteria

The vertical slice is successful if it proves that:

- the tone is correct
- the relationship between Zero2 and De’juir is emotionally resonant
- the status systems feel important and readable
- the world feels unstable and alive
- the player feels urgency without fully understanding the story
- the project has a clear path to full game expansion

## Prototype deliverables

- 1 playable opening chapter
- 1–2 core environment blocks
- 1 major memory event
- 1 major instability event
- 1 initial encounter system
- 1 supporting companion interaction loop
- 1 final chapter hook

## Recommended production priority

This slice should be built before any full world expansion. It is the proof that the story can become a game.
